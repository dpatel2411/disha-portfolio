Overview

Led a cross-functional migration initiative to transition enterprise source control repositories from Perforce to GitLab, improving scalability, developer workflows, and long-term maintainability across engineering teams.

The project involved coordinating engineers, platform teams, and external technical specialists while managing migration planning, dependency tracking, communication, risk mitigation, and execution timelines.

The Challenge

The organization relied on Perforce for source control management across hundreds of depots. As engineering needs evolved, teams required a more scalable and modern platform that better supported collaboration, automation, and future development workflows.

The migration introduced several challenges:

  • Over 200 repositories required migration
  • Existing developer workflows could not be heavily disrupted
  • Repository structures and permissions needed validation
  • Multiple engineering stakeholders depended on migration accuracy
  • Cutover planning required coordination across technical teams
  • Risk of data inconsistency or downtime during migration

The project required balancing technical execution with stakeholder coordination, operational continuity, and phased delivery planning.

My Role

As the Project Manager, I was responsible for driving coordination and delivery across the migration lifecycle.

Key Responsibilities

  • Managed project planning, timelines, milestones, and dependency tracking
  • Coordinated efforts between internal engineers and external GitLab specialists
  • Facilitated stakeholder communication and migration readiness reviews
  • Tracked risks, blockers, and migration progress across repositories
  • Supported migration scheduling and cutover coordination
  • Maintained visibility through status reporting and delivery tracking
  • Helped align technical execution with business and operational priorities

Execution Approach

01 Discovery & Planning

Worked with engineering teams to understand repository structures, migration dependencies, sequencing requirements, and operational constraints.

Defined migration phases, delivery timelines, ownership models, and communication workflows to support controlled execution.

02 Cross-Functional Coordination

Partnered with engineering teams, DevOps resources, and GitLab Professional Services to coordinate migration activities and resolve technical dependencies.

Maintained alignment across stakeholders through regular checkpoints, progress tracking, and escalation management.

03 Migration Management

Supported phased migration execution across repositories while monitoring progress, blockers, and validation checkpoints.

Coordinated scheduling and communication to reduce disruption to engineering workflows during cutovers.

04 Delivery Visibility

Established structured reporting and tracking mechanisms to maintain transparency around migration status, risks, timelines, and completion progress.

Focused on keeping stakeholders informed without overwhelming teams with unnecessary process overhead.

Outcomes

  • Successfully coordinated migration of 200+ Perforce depots to GitLab
  • Improved long-term scalability and maintainability of source control workflows
  • Helped streamline collaboration between engineering and platform teams
  • Reduced delivery friction through structured migration planning and phased execution
  • Maintained delivery momentum across a multi-month technical transformation initiative
